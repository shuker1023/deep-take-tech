AI LABS

A mystery model called Gestalt tops a new design benchmark

Contra Labs’ new Frontier Design Benchmark puts a stealth model named Gestalt at 1,931 Elo — 218 points clear of Claude Fable 5.2 (1,713), and ahead of the human reference designs (1,739). Claude Opus 5.5 (1,691), GPT-6.1 Sol (1,668) and Kimi K3.1 Pro (1,663) cluster above 1,600; DeepSeek V4 Pro trails at 1,121. Nobody has claimed Gestalt, and the scores are the benchmark maker’s own — a leaderboard is only as good as its test, and this one is brand new.

CHIPS

Congress publishes the H200 buyer list: 879,868 chips approved

The House Foreign Affairs Committee released BIS licence data on Nov 19: of 3,444,160 chips Chinese firms applied for, 879,868 were approved under 21 licences — Tencent 150,000, Alibaba 75,000, Baidu 43,000, BYD 4,000. All four are on the Pentagon’s list of Chinese military companies. The catch: approval is not delivery — none had shipped as of May 11, and Nvidia says H200 shipments to China have now stopped.

MARKETS

CrowdStrike in talks to buy Zscaler

CrowdStrike is in talks to acquire Zscaler, a deal that would combine two cybersecurity giants worth about $265B. Zscaler jumped about 17% after hours; CrowdStrike fell about 8%. Talks are ongoing and a deal is not certain — but if it lands, it would be one of the largest software mergers ever, and a bet that AI-era security belongs to whoever owns the endpoint and the network together.
