AI IPO

Anthropic prices the $1.4T IPO

Anthropic set its IPO range at $70–$78 a share — 825M shares, up to ~$64.35B raised — with Google and Nvidia each buying $10B and AMD $2.5B in a $26B private placement. Pricing Nov 18, trading Nov 19 as ANT. Run-rate revenue hit $81B — against $900.9B in total spending commitments.

AI POLICY

Claude’s door closes on China — Nov 12

Anthropic’s updated Supported Regions Policy takes effect Nov 12: mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau are not supported regions. It covers Claude.ai, Claude Code, the API and models via cloud platforms and resellers — and judges location, incorporation and ownership, so a VPN doesn’t fix it.

CHIPS

AMD keeps climbing as Nvidia slides again

AMD closed Nov 10 at $515.21 (+1.30%) after agreeing to invest $2.5B in Anthropic. Nvidia fell 5.47% to $168.89 — down 9.5% in two sessions as its board investigation drags on. Broadcom rose 3.91% the same day.
